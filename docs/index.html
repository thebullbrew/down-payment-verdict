The method

Three rules. First, reserves beat percentages. A 20% down payment that leaves you with $900 and a 40-year-old furnace isn't smart, it's theatre. Second, PMI is a rent you pay for your own money — price it in full, to the month it dies, not as a vague annoyance. Third, cash has a job. Every extra dollar down is a dollar that can't be invested, can't fix a roof, and can't carry you through a bad quarter. If it doesn't beat what that dollar earns outside the house, it stays outside the house.
